Where to stay

We have rooms at The Park, Park Street, Kolkata, for the nights of 20, 21 and 22 November.

Flying in

The airport is Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International (CCU).

It is roughly 30–45 minutes from Park Street, and 60–90 minutes from Joka, depending on traffic.

Getting around

Uber and Ola work across the city.

Joka is the last stop on the Metro Purple Line.

Weather

Late November in Kolkata is usually 18–29°C (65–84°F), dry and sunny.

Visas

Most nationalities can apply for an India e-Visa online. It is not instant, so please apply well ahead.

Write to us if you need an invitation letter for your application.
